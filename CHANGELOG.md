# Changelog

## 0.0.2-beta

- **Android 10 and later** are now supported (was Android 16): most Android TV devices and boxes
  in use can run the launcher.
- Wallpaper blur needs Android 12 or later: on older releases the option is hidden.
- PS3 theme import is hardened against malformed or oversized theme files.

## 0.0.1-beta

First public beta.

- Cross media bar home screen for Android TV: Settings, Photo, Music, Video, Games and Apps columns,
  with automatic app placement.
- Hide columns and move any app to another column. App options (open, move, app info, uninstall)
  with a long press on OK.
- Theme customization: monthly or fixed theme color, five background animations, custom wallpaper
  with blur and brightness, day/night cycle, live preview.
- PS3 theme import (`.p3t`): the theme's HD wallpaper, its system icons (GIM) for columns and
  settings, and its name and author. Wallpaper and icons can be switched independently at any time.

Required Android 16 (API 36) or later.
